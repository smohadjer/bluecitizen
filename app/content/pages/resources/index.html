Resources

This section brings together a collection of materials, ideas, and formats that help shape a cosmopolitan perspective on our relationship with the ocean.

From books and texts to videos, a documentary about our project, and creative works, these resources reflect the many ways in which ocean knowledge can be shared, experienced, and understood.

As you explore this section, you will find selected recommendations, visual and narrative works, and media contributions, as well as news about our work and related materials — including images, articles, and further resources we consider worth sharing.

Together, they offer different entry points into the idea of Blue Citizenship and Cosmopolitan Thinking — combining science, storytelling, and public dialogue.

We see these resources as part of a shared learning journey — open, evolving, and shaped by many voices.
