Compass

The Blue Citizen Compass is officially endorsed as a United Nations Ocean Decade Activity under the UN Decade of Ocean Science for Sustainable Development.

Download the Blue Citizen Compass (PDF)

The Blue Citizen Compass is one of the central outcomes of the Young Citizens' Council for the Ocean. It is a collectively developed orientation that defines what it means to be a Blue Citizen — bringing together shared values, responsibilities, policy recommendations, and ideas for institutional participation in ocean governance.

Developed through dialogue, debate, and democratic decision-making, the Compass reflects a youth-led vision of how we can relate to the ocean — not only as an ecosystem, but as a shared political space.

As you explore this section, you can dive into the different parts of the Compass — from its definition and guiding principles to concrete policy proposals and institutional ideas.

If you share these values and want to support the vision behind the Compass, we invite you to add your voice.
